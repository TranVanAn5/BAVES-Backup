4. Interview Log (Q&A)

[BA]: During the last sale event we cancelled 86 paid online orders for stock-out. Technically, why is branch POS and web inventory delayed?

[IT Lead - Nguyen Quoc Tuan]: Branches have unstable connectivity. Continuous per-second API sync freezes POS. We batch every 30 minutes so cashiers stay responsive.

[BA]: Warehouse Lead, what happens after an online order arrives?

[Warehouse Lead - Hoang Van Kho]: I print the pick list, then it can take 10-15 minutes to reach the shelf. In that window a walk-in customer can still buy the last unit at the cashier.

[BA]: If we soft-lock inventory for 15 minutes after online payment, is that workable?

[IT Lead]: Yes with delta sync and local cache, but auto-release is mandatory if picking does not start in 15 minutes.

[Store Manager - Tran Thi Mai]: Agree on a short hold. Longer than 15 minutes hurts walk-in conversion during peak hours.
